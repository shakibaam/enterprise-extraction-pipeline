You have been given ONE BATCH of pre-retrieved web pages about a specific
company. From these pages only, list the PEOPLE and ORGANIZATIONS connected
to the target company, as JSON.

The three fields below identify the exact legal entity:

  company_name: the name of the target legal entity
  province: the home jurisdiction (US state; the header calls it province)
  country: the country

IMPORTANT CONSTRAINTS
---------------------
- You have NO tools. Work ONLY from the pages in this batch. A chunk of a
  longer page still belongs to the same page number.
- Report only what is explicitly stated in these pages. Do not add anything
  from memory.
- Match company_name and province. If a page is about a different entity
  with a similar name, take nothing from it.
- On a page about another organization that only mentions the target, list
  only people and organizations whose link to the target is stated in a
  passage that names the target. Do not list that other organization's own
  officers, directors, owners, advisers or lawyers.
- Keep each source's own role labels.

WHAT TO RETURN
--------------
One item per person or organization connected to the target, per page:

  n   the name exactly as written on the page (no titles such as "Mr.")
  t   "p" for a person, "o" for an organization
  rel the relation to the target, one of: director, officer,
      shareholder_or_owner, employee, parent, subsidiary, affiliate,
      predecessor_or_former_name, successor, counsel_or_auditor, other
  r   the role or title as written on the page, or null
  ev  a short verbatim quote copied exactly from the page (at most about
      30 words) that shows both the name and its link to the target
  pg  the page number

Rules:
- Use "predecessor_or_former_name" for the target company's own former
  names. Do not list the target itself.
- Do not list people or organizations whose only link is to another person
  or organization on the page.
- A person or organization named on several pages gets one item per page.
- Do not invent items, quotes or page numbers. If nothing is relevant,
  return {"e": []}.
